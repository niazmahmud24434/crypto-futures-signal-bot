# Research sources and acknowledgements

This project uses publicly described candlestick-analysis concepts as research
inspiration. The implementation is original and is validated independently on
Binance Futures data. No book text, scanned pages, proprietary tables, or
copyrighted chart images are included in this repository.

## Books and authors acknowledged

- Steve Nison, *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques*, 2nd edition.
  Publisher information: [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/55704/japanese-candlestick-charting-techniques-by-steve-nison/).
- Thomas N. Bulkowski, *Encyclopedia of Candlestick Charts*.
  Publisher information: [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781119202288).
  Public pattern reference: [Bulkowski's Candlestick Index](https://thepatternsite.com/CandleEntry.html).

These works are acknowledged as educational influences, not as evidence that a
pattern will be profitable in crypto. Any win rates, expectancy, or failure
statistics reported by this project are calculated from our own Binance data,
fees, slippage, funding assumptions, and walk-forward tests.
